HOW TO PLAY

  1. Choose Manual to attack any visible word, or Auto for the nearest threat.
  2. Type the letters in order. Finish the word before the monster reaches the castle.
  3. Build a combo for bigger scores and use powers when the wave gets crowded.

RULES

  • Every breach costs 1 castle HP.
  • Longer words and tougher enemies award more points.
  • Special enemies trigger an area blast when destroyed.
